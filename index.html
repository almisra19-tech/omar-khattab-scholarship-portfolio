MOTIVATION LETTER · DRAFT

I am applying to study Physical Culture because I want to deepen my understanding of sports science, physical performance and athlete development. My involvement in football has made me curious about the preparation behind performance: how fitness is developed, how training methods are selected, and how athletes can reduce the risk of injury.

I hope to pursue higher education in Russia to gain a rigorous academic foundation and broaden my perspective on physical education and sport. I am particularly interested in learning how scientific principles can inform practical training and support football players' development.

After my studies, I intend to apply the knowledge and experience I gain to sports and football development in Bangladesh. I would like to continue learning and contribute in realistic, practical ways to athlete preparation and the development of young players.

Omar Khattab Bangladesh
